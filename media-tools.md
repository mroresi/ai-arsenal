# Media tools

Compression, downloads, and Mac utilities. The honest picks from the "hidden internet" listicle.

- [Squoosh](https://squoosh.app) - Google's image compressor. Runs fully locally in the browser; drag in an image, pick a codec, watch the bytes shrink.
- [Cobalt](https://cobalt.tools) ([repo](https://github.com/imputnet/cobalt)) - media downloader, 43k stars. Paste a link, get the file. Main instance verified working.
- [MacTap](https://github.com/jaskirat1616/mactap-app) - turns knocks on the MacBook chassis into shortcuts using the motion sensor. Fully local, no mic. Hardware caveat: needs M2 or later, or M1 Pro/Max/Ultra. Does NOT work on base M1 or any desktop Mac, including the M4 mini. Check the MacBook's chip first.
- [Textream](https://github.com/f/textream) - voice-tracking teleprompter. Also on Homebrew.
- Zsky AI - 50 free AI image generations per day, no signup, documented by its founder. The only honest piece of the "secret AI video sites" post; the "unlimited free" claim around it was false.
