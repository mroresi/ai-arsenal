# Self-hosted AI

Run it on your own hardware. Docker Compose on whitebox per the existing convention.

- [Paperless-ngx](https://github.com/paperless-ngx/paperless-ngx) - document management system. The standout: directly attacks the document chaos problem (blackbox inbox). The rare "stop paying" claim that actually holds.
- [Open WebUI](https://github.com/open-webui/open-webui) - user-friendly LLM frontend; sits in front of Ollama or OpenAI-compatible APIs.
- [Nhost](https://github.com/nhost/nhost) - open-source Firebase alternative: Postgres, GraphQL, auth, serverless. For when a project actually needs a backend.
- [Penpot](https://github.com/penpot/penpot) - open-source design and prototyping platform. The Figma replacement.
- [Open Notebook](https://github.com/lfnovo/open-notebook) - local AI research assistant; RAG over your own docs. Fits the research workflow.
- [Strix](https://github.com/usestrix/strix) - AI-assisted application security tool, multi-agent. Directly relevant to the team.
- [Open-kritt](https://github.com/Kritt-ai/open-kritt) - AI vulnerability scanning, self-hosted agent-orchestrated.
